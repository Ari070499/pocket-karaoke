If it sounds soft or late

  • Press your phone's volume-up buttons while you're live. Once the mic is on, phones switch to a separate volume level, so turn it up again.
  • Raise Mic boost above, and sing close to the phone's bottom edge, where the mic is.
  • For the least delay, use an AUX cable to the speaker if it has an input jack. Bluetooth alone adds about 150–250 ms that no app can remove.
